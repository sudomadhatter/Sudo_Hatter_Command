The board's lens charters are written for product and engineering work — Ground Truth means
telemetry and users, Unconventional Leverage means capability you cannot buy or hire. On a
**personal or human topic** (2026-08-26: parenting), mapping those charters across by analogy is
itself the borrowed-analogy failure the First Principles lens exists to catch. Roughly half the
roster goes inert and nothing in the command says so.

Daniel pushed back twice in one session — "we need 5 teams for this?" and then "I didnt like the
orchestrator went with the 5 teams for this either they did not apply at all here."

**Why:** casting for coverage instead of load-bearing relevance buries him in cards that restate
each other, which is the exact failure the "when NOT to seat" clauses in
`.agents/commands/adviser-board/TEAMS.md` exist to prevent. He should not have to make that cut.

**How to apply:** on a non-product topic, seat **2–3 lenses** and name which are observing and what
their absence costs. Apply each charter's *when NOT to seat* clause **before** writing the cast
gate, not after pushback. Ask the one or two facts that actually block the board at Step 0 (for a
person: age, and what they are concretely good at) rather than leaving them to a card's
`COULDN'T SETTLE`. Also: "render cards verbatim" governs the **words**, not the presentation —
keep every word, but give them headers and spacing, and pull the reframes to the top. See
[[writes-for-big-picture-operator]] and [[adviser-board-caucus-card-contract]].
