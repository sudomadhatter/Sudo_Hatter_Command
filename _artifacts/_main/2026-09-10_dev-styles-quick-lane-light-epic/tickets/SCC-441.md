Why: The project layer has one shape for every story. A non-critical UI fix pays the same ceremony as an auth change: red tests, self-audit, BDD lock, a review verdict with a roster gate, receipts, and a four-check CI run with ~16 min of Playwright on every frontend landing into an epic that is not in production. The two lanes that should be lighter are mis-shaped: `/cicd-quick-dev` skips the red test and keeps the mandatory review, the inverse of what a quick lane should drop, and the lobby's names do not match the project's (`/smh-quick-dev` IS the full TDD lane, `/smh-quick-fix` is the light one). The `-quickdev` epic mode was never used and the epic ruleset would refuse its direct push. Measured 2026-09-10 on AVCH-149: the ceremony, not the tests, cost half the day. Agreed design (operator, 2026-09-10): two independent toggles. The LANE you call (quick = TDD with the plan and walkthrough approvals kept, no self-audit and no review verdict unless asked, a soft scope check first that only the operator overrides) and the EPIC you are on (light = landing PRs run only the two fast checks, E2E once at ship or on request; the mode is the third token, `epic/<KEY>-light-epic-<N>-<slug>`). The rails never toggle: worktree per lane, key on the branch, explicit paths, `main` only by a PR the operator merges.
