## What this is

The lobby already has both lanes, under names that contradict the project's: `/smh-quick-dev` is
the full TDD lane (plan, audit, `approved`, RED, GREEN, mandatory review) and `/smh-quick-fix` is
the light one. Same words at both levels or every agent reads the wrong door. Two moves:

1. **Rename, no behaviour change.** `smh-quick-dev.md` → `smh-dev-task-tests.md` by `git mv`. The
   only lines that change are the front-matter `description:`, the title (line 6) and the
   flow-position line (line 39). The name `smh-dev-task-tests` has zero hits anywhere today.
2. **The new `/smh-quick-dev` is the quick lane**, the same five steps as the rebuilt
   `/cicd-quick-dev` (SCC-444), at lobby level. `smh-quick-fix.md` is **deleted** (ask-first:
   this plan is the ask); its `INDEX.md` row becomes the pointer.

Design record: [SCC-441 implementation_plan.md](../2026-09-10_dev-styles-quick-lane-light-epic/implementation_plan.md).
