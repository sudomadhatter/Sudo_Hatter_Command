Why: # SCC-416 — Decide the epic's mode at kickoff; freeze main for a live epic's scope

## Why

On 2026-09-05 Epic 24 work reached live production mid-epic. AVCH-80 (the rolling bug ticket) was cut as `chore/AVCH-80-rolling-bugs` off `main`; its riders were Epic 24 findings routed out of story 24.4. Its diff shared three runtime files with the live `epic/AVCH-100-epic-24-agent-quality`. `ship_preflight.py` classed it "chore touching backend → light gate → ship", `/cicd-push-e2e` opened PR #72 into `main`, it merged at `4afaa667`, and Cloud Run revision `00076-boh` took 100% of traffic. Not one Epic 24 story had been merged to `main`, by design.

The operator's ruling (2026-09-03, the Epic 24 banner in `sprint-status.yaml`, on the epic branch) forbids exactly this; a main-bound lane never reads that file. Neither preflight looks at the live branch list, and nothing records what KIND of epic a branch is: the AVCH-119 ruleset treats every `epic/**` the same, and the story door still lands by a direct push that ruleset now refuses.

Operator design: when the epic branch is cut, decide once whether it is an EXTENSION OF MAIN (full gate, E2E on every story PR, kept current with main) or a QUICK-DEV branch (light gate, direct-push landings, no CI per story, E2E once at the end). Both exist. The decision is carried in the branch name (`-quickdev` suffix) so GitHub's ruleset and every local door read the same switch. In both modes, main is frozen for the epic's scope while it lives.
