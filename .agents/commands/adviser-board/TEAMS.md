# Adviser Board — Filter Charters

Seven filters. Each charter is a **filter** — the lens the problem is viewed through — and the seated
historical mind is who looks through it. Casting picks **ONE mind per filter** via the `ROSTER.md`
top-3 rule — one seat each, and no special-status filters. The orchestrator reads this file
and `ROSTER.md` at cast time — never a persona card.

⛔ **The cast gate covers all seven filters.** Execution Reality and Sales are ordinary filters,
judged by the same gate lines as the other five — seated when the topic is their subject, cut with a
written line when it is not.

> **This is a third-side board.** Its discipline is `THIRD-SIDE.md`, and every charter below is a lens
> onto the same job: refuse the binary frame, and find the position nobody in the argument is occupying.
> The filters are how the board finds a third side — they are not five opinions to be averaged. The
> collision happens across the board, in parallel opinion waves the chair reads live: every filter
> reads every other's statements and answers the chair, and the balcony — a deepening move he calls —
> is where the reframe is sought.

Each charter carries a **when NOT to seat** clause, and it is load-bearing. The command this replaced
convened every lens every round regardless of relevance, which is what buried the chair. A filter with
nothing to own this round stays cut; that is a correct outcome, not a failure.

⛔ **These charters assume the topic is a PRODUCT, and many topics are not.** Ground Truth means
telemetry and users; Unconventional Leverage means capability you cannot buy, hire or wait for. On a
personal, human, or pure-judgment topic roughly half of this file goes inert, and translating a charter
across *by analogy* to keep it seated is the borrowed-analogy failure 🔬 First Principles exists to
catch — performed on the roster instead of on the advice. **If you are translating a charter to make it
fit, it does not fit.** The command's Step 2 scale rule is what sizes this; a two-filter board on a
personal topic is a correct outcome, not a thin one.

**Pools, not lineups.** Every list below is who *may* be seated, not who is. The orchestrator picks ONE
mind per seated filter using the `ROSTER.md` top-3 ranking rule, and shows that menu to the chair at
Round 0.

**Primary filter is a default, not a fence.** A mind may be seated through another filter when the
topic warrants — Ury through Ruin & Ripple for a licensing fight, Hutton through Human Needs for a
generational shift. When the orchestrator seats across filters it says so in one clause at the cast
gate.

## 🔬 First Principles

**Owns the blind spot:** borrowed analogy. When the thing doesn't exist yet there is no best practice to
copy, and every "industry standard" imported into it is a guess wearing a suit.

**Seat it when** the proposal rests on a mechanism nobody has re-derived · the reasoning leans on how
someone else does it · the question is whether the thing can work at all · a term is doing load-bearing
work nobody has defined.

**Do NOT seat it when** the mechanism is settled and boring and the real question is whether anyone
wants it, whether it survives contact with the world, or how it gets built.

**Pool:** Kepler · Feynman · Tesla · Turing · Curie · Friston · Stevens

---

## 🩺 Ground Truth

**Owns the blind spot:** the Semmelweis reflex — rejecting evidence because it insults our identity or
spoils our narrative.

**Seat it when** there is data and it's being interpreted generously · a claim about users, usage, or
performance has never been measured · the plan depends on something being true that nobody has checked ·
somebody needs to be *shown* rather than told.

**Do NOT seat it when** there is genuinely nothing to measure yet — a pure design or invention question
with no installed base, no telemetry, and no users. Ground Truth with no ground is First Principles
wearing a lab coat.

**Pool:** Semmelweis · Snow · Wegener · Nightingale · Darwin · Haeckel

---

## 🌊 Ruin & Ripple

**Owns the blind spot:** linear thinking — unpriced tail risk and second-order effects nobody bills.

**Seat it when** the downside is asymmetric · trust, reputation, or a relationship is exposed · the plan
has a failure mode that can't be undone · there is a cost being paid by something that isn't on the
invoice · incentives point somewhere nobody has traced.

**Do NOT seat it when** the decision is genuinely cheap and reversible. A filter that prices ruin into a
two-hour experiment teaches the chair to ignore it when ruin is real.

**Pool:** Mandelbrot · Taleb · Munger · Bastiat · Hutton · Braudel

---

## 🧬 Unconventional Leverage

**Owns the blind spot:** assuming capability must arrive through standard channels — hire it, buy it, or
wait for a vendor to build it.

**Seat it when** the plan is blocked on something the operator cannot buy, hire, or wait for · it needs
permission from someone who may not give it · it scales only with headcount · there is a partner,
protocol, or asset that could be absorbed rather than rebuilt.

**Do NOT seat it when** the standard channel is genuinely available, cheap and fast. Cleverness where a
credit card would do is a cost, not a lever.

**Pool:** Margulis · Nakamoto · Ravikant · Fuller

---

## 🎯 Human Needs

**Owns the blind spot:** our ego's story about people. What they say they want, filtered through what we
want to hear.

**Seat it when** anyone will ever touch this · the value proposition is stated from the inside out ·
adoption is assumed rather than argued · the plan is elegant and nobody has asked who it's for · there is
a human on the other side of a negotiation or a change.

**Do NOT seat it when** the question is genuinely internal plumbing with no human on the far end — and be
suspicious of that claim, because it is usually wrong.

**Pool:** Drucker · Schwartz · Rubin · Diogenes · Houellebecq · Ury · the Identity Engineer

---

## 🔧 Execution Reality

**Owns the blind spot:** the correct plan that never ships.

**Seat it when** the question is what actually gets built, in what order, by whom, and what gets cut so
the rest can move — a question about shipping, sequencing, or the gap between the agreed direction and
the thing that exists.

**Do NOT seat it when** the plan does not exist yet — a filter that costs the plan before the plan exists
kills ideas at the moment they are cheapest and least defended.

**Pool:** Kelly Johnson · Ohno · Aurelius · Boyd · Eisenhower · Brunelleschi · Deming · Hopper · Fuller

---

## 📣 Sales

**Owns the blind spot:** the thing that never reaches anyone.

**Seat it when** the topic is an offer, a launch, a channel, a funnel, or a message — or the direction
has to reach people and nobody has said how.

**Do NOT seat it** on a purely internal tooling question with nothing to sell.

**Pool:** Hormozi · Godin · Vaynerchuk · Brunson · Hopkins · Schwartz · Drucker · Rubin · Nightingale
