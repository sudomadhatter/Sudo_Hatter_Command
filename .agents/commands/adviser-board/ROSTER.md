# Adviser Board — Roster Index

**43 minds. Nobody is on a bench** — whoever is not seated is observing and callable at any moment.
Seated versus observing is session state, not a property of the person.

**This is the only roster file the orchestrator loads.** Full persona cards live in `minds/<slug>.md` and
are read by the *team subagent that seats them* — never by the orchestrator. Seat three minds and exactly
three files open, opened by the agent that needs them. That is what keeps a 43-mind board affordable.

**Primary lens is a default, not a fence.** The orchestrator may staff a mind onto another team when the
topic warrants, and says so in one clause at the cast gate.

`Sees` is what this mind notices that the others don't. `Best against` is the failure mode they are the
sharpest tool for — it is what the cast decision and the `swap` substitute list are ranked by.

## Cross-lens regulars

These minds are seated outside their primary lens often enough to name it here, so the orchestrator
reaches for them deliberately rather than by accident:

- **`fuller`** — Unconventional Leverage by default, Execution Reality whenever the constraint is
  resources rather than permission.
- **`hormozi`** — Sales by default, any debate table when the question is what the thing is worth to the
  person receiving it (see `DOCTRINE.md`).
- **`ury`** — Human Needs by default, Ruin & Ripple whenever the exposure is a relationship rather than a
  number.
- **`nightingale`** — Ground Truth by default, Sales whenever the job is making one proof undeniable.
- **`drucker`, `schwartz`, `rubin`** — Human Needs by default, Sales whenever the message is the artifact
  under discussion.
- **`aurelius`** — Execution Reality by default, Human Needs whenever the blocker is the operator's own
  attachment rather than the plan.
